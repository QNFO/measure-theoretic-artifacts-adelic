## 3. QNFO's Genuine Novelty

The programme's unique contributions not covered in any existing p-adic physics literature:

1. **Systematic completion-failure catalog**: 21 predictions in 4 tiers, each classified by cosmetic vs. non-cosmetic status. No external paper has attempted this categorization.

2. **Product formula as falsifiable constraint**: D2 computes explicit constraints (∏|σ̂_p|_p = 60/π², ∏|C_p|_p = 240/π², ∏|α_p|_p = 137.036) that can be tested without building a p-adic laboratory.

3. **Causality analysis**: C1-RT is the first systematic comparison of 4 frameworks (Page-Wootters, Wheeler-DeWitt, Superdeterminism, Bruhat-Tits) for p-adic causal structure.

4. **Quantified O(1) differences**: A1, A2, A5 provide explicit numerical comparisons between Haar measure coefficients and Archimedean solid-angle factors — the external literature states there is a difference but does not quantify it.

5. **Directional inversion of magnitude insight**: The Archimedean "more = bigger" is the unique exception — every p-adic place inverts this (|p^k|_p = p^{-k} → 0). This is the structural fact that makes the product formula possible. Not present in any external literature in this formulation.
