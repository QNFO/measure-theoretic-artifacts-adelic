## Adversary 1: Null-Hypothesis Defender

**Role:** "Nothing new here — Archimedean-only physics works perfectly. Status quo explains everything."

### 1.1 Claim Under Review
> "The adelic product formula imposes constraints that single-place analysis cannot see" (programme thesis).

### 1.2 Challenge

The product formula ∏_v |x|_v = 1 is a theorem about rational numbers. It says: for any x ∈ ℚ^×, the product of all normalized absolute values equals 1. This is mathematically true. But physics uses ℝ and ℂ — not ℚ.

**The entire programme rests on a category error:** it treats physical constants as if they were elements of ℚ (and therefore subject to Ostrowski's theorem), but the Standard Model defines them as elements of ℝ or ℂ. A fine-structure constant α = 1/137.036 is a real number, not a rational number. Its p-adic "completions" under Ostrowski only exist if α is defined over ℚ — which it is not.

**Formal objection:** If α ∈ ℝ\ℚ (which it is — α is a measured real number, not defined as a ratio of integers), then there is no p-adic analog α_p ∈ ℚ_p defined by the completion procedure. Ostrowski's theorem classifies completions of ℚ — it says nothing about completions of ℝ (which only has one: ℂ). The programme's claim that "α has p-adic components" is mathematically ill-defined.

**Counter-response (from programme):** The programme argues that physics SHOULD be defined over ℚ, not ℝ — that ℚ is the correct base field, and ℝ is a projection. But this is a normative claim (how physics should be), not a descriptive one (how physics is). The burden of proof is on the programme to show that ℚ-based physics is physically distinct from ℝ-based physics. Phase 2 does not provide this evidence — it only shows WHAT would differ IF the base field were ℚ.

### 1.3 Verdict

| Criterion | Score (1-5) |
|:---|:---:|
| Force of objection | 5 — This is the strongest objection against the programme |
| Programme's response strength | 2 — Normative claim not backed by evidence |
| Remains answerable? | Yes — but requires an argument for ℚ > ℝ that the programme doesn't have |

**Null hypothesis stands.** The programme has not overcome the objection that physics uses ℝ/ℂ, not ℚ.

## Adversary 2: Methodology Skeptic

**Role:** "Your method is flawed. The classification is circular and the 'failure' catalog is engineered to produce the desired conclusion."

### 2.1 Circularity in the 4-Tier Classification

The programme classifies predictions as "non-cosmetic" when they differ between Archimedean and p-adic completions. But:

1. **The classification predates the computation.** A prediction is labeled Tier 1 (numerically non-cosmetic) because it CONTAINS π. Then the p-adic analog is computed and found to differ. But the difference is guaranteed by the presence of π — which is exactly what the classification identified. There is no test here, only a definition.

2. **"Non-cosmetic" is defined as "completion-dependent."** The programme then shows that π-dependent predictions ARE completion-dependent (π doesn't exist in ℚ_p). This is a tautology: predictions that depend on π are π-dependent. The number 21 is an artifact of the selection criteria, not a property of physics.

3. **The 21-prediction catalog is not an objective list.** It was constructed by (a) identifying formulas containing π, e, exp, or continuous geometry, (b) noting that these don't exist in ℚ_p, and (c) counting 21 occurrences. The scope was predetermined to confirm the thesis.

### 2.2 The "Tier 4" Escape Hatch

Tier 4 (borderline/ambiguous) includes α (the fine-structure constant), g−2, the Lamb shift, and the CC hierarchy — some of the most physically significant quantities. These are classified Tier 4 precisely because the programme's framework struggles to make clean predictions about them:

| Quantity | Tier | Convenient property |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| Stefan-Boltzmann | 1 | Contains π explicitly |
| Casimir | 1 | Contains π explicitly |
| Fine-structure α | 4 | Might be cosmetic — no clean π-dependence |
| CC hierarchy | 4 | Speculative — can't be falsified yet |

This creates an appearance of rigor ("21 predictions, only 4 are ambiguous") that is actually a selection bias ("We studied the 17 predictions that were easiest to classify.")

### 2.3 Verdict

| Criterion | Score |
|:---|:---:|
| Circularity risk | 4 — Real, but partially mitigated by certainty labels |
| Selection bias | 3 — Honest about scope, but cumulative effect is misleading |
| Can be addressed? | Yes — add a statement: "All Tier 1-3 predictions share the property of containing π explicitly. This is not a coincidence — it's the selection criterion." |

## Adversary 4: Scaling Pessimist

**Role:** "Phase 3 requires PGL(n) building theory that nobody has ever applied to physics problems at the level required. This won't scale."

### 4.1 The Mathematical Wall

Phase 3 tasks require:

| Task | Mathematical Prerequisites | Existing Physics Implementation | Feasibility |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| C1-RT.2: Tree Mellin amplitudes | p-adic analysis, tree traversal, geometric series | NOT DONE — some theory papers (Zabrodin 1989) | MODERATE — 2-3 weeks Python dev |
| C1-RT.3: PGL(3-4) buildings | Bruhat-Tits building theory, spherical functions, chamber-walk distances | NOT DONE — NOT in physics literature | VERY LOW — requires research-level mathematics |
| C1-RT.4: Adelic S-matrix | Restricted direct products, p-adic representation theory | NOT DONE | LOW — depends on C1-RT.2, C1-RT.3 |
| C1-RT.5: Page-Wootters clock | Adelic quantum theory, conditional states L²(A_ℚ) | NOT DONE | LOW — requires novel framework |

**The programme understates Phase 3 difficulty.** C1-RT.2 is tractable (2-3 weeks Python). C1-RT.3 is NOT — PGL(3) building spherical functions and chamber-walk Green's functions are current research mathematics, not engineering. A single physics PhD student could not implement C1-RT.3 within a 3-year thesis. The claim "SPECIFIED — ready for implementation" is misleading.

### 4.2 The PGL(n) Literature Gap

The spherical function theory on buildings exists in pure mathematics (Cartier 1993, Macdonald 1972). But:
- It is expressed in the language of algebraic groups (Bruhat decomposition, Hecke algebras, intertwiners)
- No translation to physics notation exists
- The chamber-walk Green's function (the building analog of the Feynman propagator) has NEVER been computed in physics context

**To implement C1-RT.3, someone must:**
1. Master spherical function theory on PGL(3, ℚ_p) buildings
2. Derive the chamber-walk distance formula algorithmically
3. Implement in Python with convergence guarantees
4. Compare integer-spaced poles to Archimedean continuous case

This is 1-2 research-level journal papers, not an artifact write-up.

### 4.3 Verdict

| Criterion | Score |
|:---|:---:|
| Phase 3 implementability | Very Low for C1-RT.3; Moderate for C1-RT.2 |
| Programme's assessment of difficulty | Understated — "SPECIFIED" implies greater readiness than warranted |
| Should Phase 3 continue? | Yes — but C1-RT.2 (tree, tractable) should be separated from C1-RT.3-4 (building, research-level) with explicit difficulty labels |
